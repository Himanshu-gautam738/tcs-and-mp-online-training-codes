Chocolate Cake Recipe

Print-friendly version available!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Mix flour, sugar, cocoa powder, and baking powder in a bowl.
  3. Add eggs, milk, and oil. Mix until smooth.
  4. Pour into a greased baking pan.
  5. Bake for 30-35 minutes. Check with a toothpick.
  6. Let it cool before serving.
