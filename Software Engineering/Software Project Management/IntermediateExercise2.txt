Given:
Optimistic (O) = 4
Most Likely (M) = 6
Pessimistic (P) = 10

PERT Formula:
Expected Time (TE) = (O + 4M + P) / 6

Calculation:
TE = (4 + 4×6 + 10) / 6
TE = (4 + 24 + 10) / 6
TE = 38 / 6 = 6.33 days (approx)

------------------------------------------------------------

How PERT helps:

1. Handles Uncertainty:
PERT considers three estimates (O, M, P), making it more realistic.

2. Better Planning:
Gives an expected average time instead of a single fixed value.

3. Risk Analysis:
Helps identify tasks with high uncertainty.

------------------------------------------------------------

Where PERT is Suitable:

PERT is more suitable for New Product Development projects.

Reason:
- New projects have uncertainty
- Time estimation is difficult
- No past data available

In maintenance projects:
- Work is predictable
- Less uncertainty
- Fixed estimates are easier

------------------------------------------------------------

Conclusion:

PERT provides a more accurate and flexible way to estimate time in uncertain projects, especially in new development scenarios.
