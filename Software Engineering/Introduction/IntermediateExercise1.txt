Requirement gathering is the most important initial phase of the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC). 
If this phase is not performed properly, several problems can arise throughout the development process.

1. Impact on Design:
Without clear requirements, it becomes difficult to design the system architecture.
The design may be incomplete, incorrect, or not aligned with user needs.

2. Impact on Development:
Developers may not clearly understand what needs to be built.
This can lead to incorrect implementation, missing features, and frequent code changes (rework).

3. Impact on Testing:
Testing team will not have clear expected outcomes.
Test cases may be incorrect or incomplete, leading to undetected bugs and poor software quality.

4. Client Satisfaction:
The final product may not meet client expectations.
This leads to dissatisfaction, rejection of the product, or demand for major changes.

5. Project Cost:
Frequent changes and rework increase development and testing costs significantly.

6. Time Delay:
Unclear requirements cause confusion and repeated work, leading to project delays.

Real-World Example:
Consider a team developing an online food delivery application.
Due to poor requirement gathering, important features like live tracking and online payment were not included.
As a result:
- The client was not satisfied
- The system had to be redeveloped
- Both time and cost increased significantly

Conclusion:
Proper requirement gathering is essential. Without it, the project becomes inefficient, costly, and risky.
