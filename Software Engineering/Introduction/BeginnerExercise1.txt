Process, Product, and Project are three important concepts in software engineering.

1. Process:
Process is a set of activities or steps followed to develop software.
It includes planning, designing, coding, testing, and maintenance.
Example: Agile, Waterfall model.

2. Product:
Product is the final software that is delivered to the user.
It is the output of the process.
Example: Mobile apps like WhatsApp, websites like Amazon.

3. Project:
Project is the complete effort to develop a software product within a specific time and budget.
It includes resources, team, and schedule.
Example: Developing a banking system within 6 months.

Contribution in SDLC:
- Process defines how work is done.
- Product is the result of the work.
- Project manages time, cost, and resources to complete the work.
