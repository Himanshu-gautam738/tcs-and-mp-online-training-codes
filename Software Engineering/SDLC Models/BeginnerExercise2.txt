In a complex system like a Hospital Management System, following a well-defined software process model is very important.

Reasons:

1. Clear Understanding of Requirements:
The system includes multiple modules like patient records, billing, and emergency services.
A process model ensures all requirements are properly gathered and understood.

2. Better Planning and Organization:
The process model helps in planning tasks, timelines, and resources efficiently.

3. Reduced Errors:
Without a proper process, coding directly may lead to mistakes and missing features.
Structured phases help detect errors early.

4. Improved Quality:
Testing and validation are part of the process model, ensuring high-quality software.

5. Handling Complexity:
Hospital systems are complex and critical.
A process model helps manage complexity through proper design and modularization.

6. Risk Management:
Potential risks are identified early and handled properly.

7. Client Satisfaction:
Regular feedback and proper development ensure the final product meets client expectations.

Example:
If coding starts directly without planning, important features like emergency handling or secure patient data may be missed, leading to system failure.

Conclusion:
A well-defined process model ensures systematic development, reduces risk, improves quality, and delivers reliable software.
